Our History

Club Fantasy in Charleston

2010

Club Fantasy Opens — North Charleston

Club Fantasy established at 2110 Greenridge Rd, Ste B in North Charleston — entering the adult entertainment corridor that included several of the area's most active gentlemen's clubs. The venue quickly became a recognized name in the Charleston adult entertainment scene.

2015

Charleston's Bachelorette Boom

Charleston established itself as a top Southeast bachelorette destination alongside Nashville and Savannah. Demand for both club-based and private adult entertainment grew sharply across the greater Charleston area as the short-term rental market expanded.

2021

Transition to Directory

Club Fantasy SC transitioned into Charleston's adult entertainment directory — a comprehensive resource connecting people with strippers, gentlemen's clubs, and private entertainment across all nine service areas of the greater Charleston metro.

Now

Charleston's Adult Entertainment Directory

Today Club Fantasy SC serves as the greater Charleston area's complete adult entertainment guide — covering strip clubs, exotic dancers, private shows, and bachelorette entertainment across every neighborhood from downtown to the beaches and beyond.
